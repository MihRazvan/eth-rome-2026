BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Private.
With clear boundaries.

What can other people see?

Task scopes, rewards, wallets and payment history are public. The credential itself is not published with a proof. Reports are encrypted, but their size and recipient metadata remain visible. Reusing a wallet links your activity.

Who can review?

Anyone can set up reviewer access with a wallet confirmation. The private proof checks valid participation, not professional expertise or report quality. The client evaluates the work before approving payment.

Do I need a storage account or JSON files?

No storage account or credential files are required. Reviewer access is set up automatically in the app. Keep the same browser profile: your saved pass and private report keys live here. A private pass backup is optional; it does not back up report keys.

What if a review never finishes?

Escrow has explicit deadline and dispute routes. A listing disappearing from the board does not move funds. Refunds require an eligible onchain action; disputes in this test edition use the team’s arbitrator.

Can I try it without funding a wallet?

Yes. The walkthrough encrypts and decrypts a report in your browser, with simulated qualification and payment. The live exchange needs Fuji test AVAX and client test USDC. Reviewer access is created automatically when you take your first task.

    Deadlines & listing duration

    Default: one hour each for acceptance, delivery and client review. The discovery listing lasts about 30 minutes when published.
