    CUTOUT TEST ISSUER · TECHNICAL REVIEW

    A qualification comes from an issuer.

    The issuer assesses the reviewer and signs their qualification. In this hackathon demo, the Cutout team issues temporary test credentials. This is not professional accreditation, and the proof does not establish that a report is correct.

    1. Prepare your private pass. Create it in this browser and save the private backup. No wallet payment is required. Your secret stays on your device.
    2. Get issuer approval. Share only the enrollment request with the Cutout team through your existing private contact. The issuer signs a time-limited technical-review credential and returns the credential file. Creating a request does not grant qualification or send an application automatically.
    3. Prove you qualify for a task. Select the issued credential and your private holder backup on a funded task, then cut a proof. The contract checks the issuer signature, expiry and current revocation state before you can accept.

    Prepare enrollment

    Already have an issued credential and its holder backup? Use them on a task. Create a new pass only for a new enrollment.

    First save the private backup. Reloading, changing wallets or clearing this form discards this tab's copy. Never send that backup to the issuer.

    Private files are read locally for proving. The issuer receives a commitment, not your holder secret. Public payments still reveal wallet addresses.

    01 / WRITE THE BRIEF

    What needs a second look?

    Your brief is public. Your finished report is private.

    Deadlines & listing duration

    Default: one hour each for acceptance, delivery and client review. The discovery listing lasts about 30 minutes when published.

    After funding, publish a lease from the same client wallet on Arkiv. The default 900-block lease lasts about 30 minutes, shortened automatically when the task's acceptance deadline is closer. Expiry removes the listing; it does not cancel the escrow.

    After funding, publish the task to the reviewer board.

    When is the reviewer paid?

    Approve the report or dispute it before your review deadline. After that deadline, a timely report can claim payment unless disputed. The test arbitrator resolves disputes.

    What stays private?

    The credential identifier and holder secret stay in the reviewer’s browser. The issuer, qualification class, deadlines, task and payment wallet are public. Wallet reuse links tasks.

    Report-key bindings and recipient metadata are public. Revoking a key prevents new encryption to it; existing documents cannot be recalled.

    Wallet transaction receipts

      A sent transaction may still settle after a timeout. Check its hash before retrying.

      Privacy & test edition details

      This edition uses one test issuer, test assets and an experimental proof system. Qualification verifies eligibility, not report quality. Credential inputs stay in your browser; only the proof goes onchain.

      Private report keys stay in this browser profile. Keep this profile and save copies of completed reports: another device cannot open an old report without its original key.
