    How do I qualify for a review?

    This pilot trusts one test issuer. The issuer gives you a credential for the technical-review class. It does not prove that a report is correct: the client evaluates the work.

    Current setup: bring your issuer-provided credential and holder file, and run the local prover with the public setup. A team member must provision these before a first reviewer test. Do not upload those private files to a website or send them in chat.

    A proof is specific to the task and your wallet. An old proof or a revoked qualification cannot be reused for a new acceptance.

    COMMISSION A REVIEW

    Commission a review.

    Start with one specific code change or question. The scope is public; the delivered report is private. This pilot uses a test qualification issuer and valueless test assets.

    Timing and discovery options

    Default: one hour each for acceptance, delivery and client review. The discovery listing lasts about one minute when published.

    After funding, publish a lease from the same client wallet on Arkiv. A 30-block lease lasts about a minute. Expiry removes the listing; it does not cancel the escrow.

    The client approves completed work or disputes it before the review deadline. A timely submission can claim payment after that deadline unless disputed. The configured arbitrator resolves disputes.

    WHAT GETS DISCLOSED

    Issuer, qualification class, deadline, payment address and assignment are public. Credential identifier and holder secret remain inside your local prover. Reusing a wallet links assignments.

    Device-key bindings and encrypted recipient metadata are public. Revoking a key stops new encryption to it; it cannot recall old documents.
