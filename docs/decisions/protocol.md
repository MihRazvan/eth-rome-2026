# Protocol decision — immutable whole-bundle claim ownership

2026-09-11. Scope: controlled EXIT Test Withdrawal Vault, not BENQI or a production source.

The shared `ExitMarket` has an immutable vault and exact-transfer payment token. Each source request is originated by the market; only that requester can pull source payments, always to itself. An internal ownership record controls all claim cash and future recovery. There is no NFT transfer/approval callback, generic execution, delegatecall, administrator, upgrade, cancellation destination, or receipt burn. This deliberately narrows the authority surface while retaining resale through explicit signed sale.

Each purchase transfers the full supported remainder: pending/claimable source entitlement, recognized cash held in the market, and subsequently funded source recoveries. A sale increments the ownership epoch. A withdrawal increments depletion by the amount removed. Collection into claim credit and time progression change neither; unsolicited token dust is excluded from claim accounting. Even a zero remaining standard balance keeps the ownership record for future recoveries. An offer can sell a future-recovery-only remainder; the seller must explicitly accept its price.

`PurchaseQuote` uses EIP-712 domain EXIT/1, chain ID, and market address. It binds maker, current seller, buyer, claim ID, source/version, token, seller net, fee/recipient, ownership epoch/depletion, deadline, maker-scoped nonce and independent underwriting-document hash. A quote authorizes that maker's capital. Only the current seller's direct call accepts exact terms; approvals are never acceptance. Nonces are consumed or cancelled per maker. OpenZeppelin `SignatureChecker` evaluates ERC-1271 authorization at execution. `hashQuote` is the shared canonical digest for the client.

Payments verify actual debit and recipient credit, including each separate fee leg, under one reentrancy guard shared by every market mutation. Positive-fee recipients cannot alias maker, seller, or market; seller net is therefore exactly the signed net payment. Failed fee transfers revert the preceding seller payment, ownership and nonce. Tokens must be non-rebasing and transfer exact amounts; unsupported behavior is rejected during checked transfers. This admission is for the deployed immutable TestUSDC, not an assertion of arbitrary ERC-20 compatibility.

The test vault accepts full backing on origination. Forty percent matures after 60 seconds and the remainder after 120 seconds. Opt-in adverse origination irreversibly sends 3% of the initial test deposit to the fixed public `0xdead` sink; the final installment is 57%. This is a disclosed deterministic simulation of loss with actual test-token transfers and liabilities. There is no privileged time control. Anyone may fund additional recoveries, with exact backed inflows. The TestUSDC faucet is permissionless, repeatable and valueless; a visitor needs test AVAX gas independently.

`OfferKeyRegistry` separately authenticates seller/request encryption-key hash, monotonically increasing version and validity. Direct seller registration/revocation prevents substituted or stale registry state being authenticated by another account. Clients must check both the wallet certificate and fresh registry state. Revocation is not quote cancellation and does not erase previously decrypted data.

Dependencies: Solidity 0.8.30, Cancun EVM target, OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.6.1 from the integrator-owned npm lockfile, Foundry 1.5.1. Standard persistent-storage ReentrancyGuard is used. Primary API references checked: [OpenZeppelin cryptography](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/api/utils/cryptography) and [ERC-20](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/api/token/erc20). Compiler and Foundry executable probes passed locally. Fuji deployment compatibility and public transaction execution remain separate acceptance evidence.
