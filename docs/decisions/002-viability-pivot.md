# Source and market direction after viability testing

The September 11 user instruction explicitly authorizes reassessing and adjusting EXIT's approach to find a viable product. This decision supersedes the earlier assumption that BENQI/Avalanche is necessarily the best commercial launch source. It does not rewrite the supplied handoff or relabel its outstanding sponsor requirements as passed.

Decision: stop expanding the generic liquid-token/retail BENQI marketplace thesis. Investigate an embedded purchase service for **existing, noncancellable native withdrawal NFTs**, initially Lido and standard ether.fi on Ethereum. Actual route quotes, caller-bound BENQI requests, incumbent ARM distribution and current native-claim observations support this narrower test. See [full assessment](../viability/README.md).

Implement now: read-only source inspection, explicit underwriting scenarios, reproducible economic and acquisition probes, and a concrete holder/maker pilot protocol. No native-NFT settlement is enabled merely because transfer probes pass. No production price, buyer capital or customer willingness is invented. Existing local test-vault trading remains available as separately labelled technical evidence.

Retain P1–P7 as requirements for any future settlement implementation. Adapt source-specific behavior honestly: Lido and ether.fi standard requests are whole receipts with full collection/burn, not the controlled vault's partial installments. Resale here means selling the still-uncollected whole NFT; fractionalization or partial rights require a separate justified design. Payment should use the source's ETH/WETH denomination unless an explicit conversion policy is added.

A same-chain Ethereum implementation does not satisfy an Avalanche settlement claim. Keep the Fuji/sponsor evidence ledger intact; revisit bounty choice explicitly if the commercial source is pursued. Private Offers remains available in the existing product and a proposed optional RFQ capability, but its confidentiality cannot justify a trade with poor economics.

Expansion requires concrete source eligibility, willing holders, independent funded makers, sustainable accepted prices and a practical distribution/servicing path. Absence of that evidence is a reason to stop or revise, not to produce more self-trades. No partner outreach or spending is authorized by this decision itself; actual user authorization governs those actions.
